01 · Tissue mechanics & transport

How does fluid movement govern tissue behavior?

My doctoral research showed that fluid flow between cells can dominate stress relaxation in multicellular tissues. Combining mechanical measurements, imaging, and physical models, I connected intercellular spaces to tissue-level mechanical response.

Nature Physics, 2025 ↗MIT News ↗
About the tracer experiments

Green: fluid tracer (FD4); red: cell membranes; blue: nuclei. These complementary tracer experiments visualize intercellular transport. The efflux movie uses an osmotic perturbation, rather than mechanical compression. Supplementary Fig. 9.
